## Interpretation

- Landscape fixed side-view arena, with visible floor/ceiling/side-wall depth. Three platforms leave an open floor for combat.
- Echo is the original name for the third hero. All absorbed abilities last ten seconds; new successful punches replace and refresh them. Robot legs increase jump strength.
- C jumps, V attacks; arrows/WASD move, climb walls, aim and drop through platforms. Space is an extra jump shortcut. P/Esc pause.
- Sixty active simulation seconds per wave; pause and focus loss stop time. Clear waves award 1,000 points and 20 health. Enemy health/damage continue scaling indefinitely; population and speed are bounded for performance.
- Original procedural 3D geometry and SVG portraits suit the style; no generated raster art or downloaded character art is claimed.
